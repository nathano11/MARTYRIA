# MARTYRIA (Testimony)

What I saw in a vision, I have testified, and what I heard, I write to you.

There was no king on the earth, and a man and a bull made a covenant on the mountain of the Bull, and Mithras, by whom the Persians swear, was the witness.

And the man gave water and grass, and the bull gave milk and strength, and the god took the firstborn and the fat.

And they called the covenant a bond, because a bond it was. And the herd passed under the rod, and the shepherd counted it.

And wealth was born in the herd, and poverty with it, and a man was priced in cattle.

And from the north came the men in chariots, sons of the Father in heaven, and they said: "The earth has been given to us."

And it was very good, and it was a bond and a chain.

⸏

Then a serpent came out of the storehouse, where the grain was locked up and the door was shut.

The ancient dragon, with seven heads and ten horns, and on the horns were names:

the Fence, the Book of Debts, the Brand, the Story, the Shame, the Divide, the Sword, the Eye, the Pasture and the Ladder. These are the ten horns.

And I looked, and the serpent dragged the herd into the cave, and they were no longer cattle but men. And the kings took the shepherd's rod and called themselves shepherds of the people.

And in the north, the bull was the god of the brothers, at Bethel and at Dan. And the brother from the south, who had bowed to Assyria, came with fire. He burned the altar, ground the calf into powder and made them drink it, and slaughtered the priests on their own altars. And the covenant of the bull was broken.

And the brother who bowed wrote the book of the brother who fell. He called his calf a sin, and he wrote himself down as a people chosen above all nations. And where it was written, "Let them rule over the beasts of the earth," they read, "Let them rule over the sons of men."

And they ate the fat and dressed in the wool, and gave the sheep shame instead of bread.

⸏

The age runs like a chariot wheel, and the wheel never stops.

The few gather, the wealth multiplies, and the door is shut. The many cry out to one man, because he is not one of the few.

And the one man breaks the few, and his court becomes the few, and the wheel turns in blood.

And I saw the merchant who was last made first, and the priest sold for silver, and the king bought.

And the serpent changed its skin seven times: in Babel and in Egypt, in Babylon and in Rome,

in the seal of the high priest and on the throne of the merchant,

and in the tower of glass, which sees everything and neither sleeps nor slumbers.

⸏

Behold the Third, whom the Indians praise as Trita, the words of Zoroaster as Thraetaona, the Persians as Artagnes, and the Greeks as Heracles,

who drank the holy drink, went down into the cave and struck the three-headed serpent, as Bel split the sea-dragon and Michael fought the dragon,

and drove the cattle out of the rock, and did not keep them for himself.

And his sword comes out of his mouth, and his name is called the Word of God.

And the serpent showed him all the kingdoms of the world and said, "I will give you this power." And he did not take the rope. Instead he washed his brothers' feet, because the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.

And after him came a Pharisee of the tribe of Benjamin, who never knew him in the flesh. He took his words and wrote in their place, "Let every soul be subject to the higher powers," and "Slaves, obey your masters." And he gave the rope back to Caesar.

And I asked him, "Where do you live?" And he said to me, "Where two are gathered in truth and love, there I am, the third between them, and not in the temple."

⸏

Then, at the last turning of the great wheel, the serpent's skin covered the whole earth, and there was no outside. And voices went to the ends of the earth like lightning, and the fire of the sun was given into the hands of men.

And the whole earth became one storehouse and one herd. And silver became a number, and the number became their god, and they worshipped the number.

And the people said, "We are rich and need nothing," and did not know that they were wretched and poor and blind and naked. And they worshipped the work of their own hands. They honored the truth with their lips, but their hearts were far from it, and they never counted the cost.

And behold, an eye that does not sleep. And the deeds of all men are written in light, and it is not erased.

And behold, the great land of the setting sun, the daughter of the exodus, who wrote on her seal: "Resistance to tyrants is obedience to God."

And she became Babylon the Great, the mother of harlots and of the abominations of the earth. She covered the names of her own and exposed the names of the lambs. But nothing covered will stay hidden.

And the merchants of the earth will weep, because no one buys their cargo anymore, the bodies and souls of men. And the books were opened, and they were naked like little children, and not ashamed.

⸏

No king will bind the serpent, and no deathless emperor on a golden throne, because the throne gives birth to the serpent.

And this is the last turning: if the wheel turns in blood, the whole earth will burn, because there is no outside, and the fire is in their hands.

And I heard a voice say to the daughter of the exodus: "Remember where you have fallen from. Repent, and do the first works, so that you do not become the Pharaoh you drowned."

But when the many say, "We will not serve," the tyrant falls like a statue with no base, and the guards lay down their weapons.

And all the people went out to the holy mountain and sat down, and the city was emptied.

And the Third gave back the power and went home to his field. He did not reign, because he remembered that he is a man.

And I saw the cattle coming out of the cave into the light, and they were no longer cattle but men.

⸏

Sound the trumpet in the fiftieth year,

and every debt will be forgiven, and every man will return to his own land.

The slave will return to his mother, and no nation will be chosen above another, and there will be no fence between people and people.

And every man will rest under his own vine and under his own fig tree, and no one will make him afraid.

And the wheel turns, but not in blood, and truth and faith lead the way.

It is not on you to finish the work, but you are not free to walk away from it.

The kingdom is spread out over the earth, and people do not see it.

⸏

And you shall proclaim release throughout the land to all who live in it.
